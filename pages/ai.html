How the AI finds supply links

It reads the documents procurement already holds, turns them into links on the supply map, and suggests links nobody has stated yet. A suggestion is a lead to check, never a fact.

Read

Takes in what JLR and its Tier 1s already send and receive.

ContractsBOMsPCF statementsDeclarationsCustoms recordsDelivery notes
Extract

Pulls out who supplies whom, which material, how much and when. Each link keeps the sentence and page it came from.

rear cover BOMs
Match

Resolves names to one company in the supplier register, using legal name, address and registration number.

“S. L Castings GmbH” = Supplier L
Suggest

A graph model learns from confirmed links which patterns go together, such as shared alloys, shipments and similar buyers, and scores pairs that have no link yet.

Confirm

The supplier confirms or rejects through its Tier 1. Only a confirmed link enters a carbon calculation.

Three kinds of link

ConfirmedBacked by verified evidence or a signed contract.
StatedNamed in a declaration or record, not yet checked.
Suggested ✦No document states it. The model found the pattern.
✦The match score ranks which suggestion to check first. It is not the probability that the link is true.
